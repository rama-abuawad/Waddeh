# Prompts for GPT Image Generation

## Global Direction

Create a cohesive set of three portrait images for the desktop slideshow on the Waddeh authentication page.

Waddeh is an adaptive Arabic reading companion. Its core story is: Understand -> Learn -> Adapt -> Progress. The visuals should communicate that Waddeh helps learners move from clear, controlled Arabic toward authentic Arabic while preserving meaning and building vocabulary memory.

All three images should feel like one family: same lighting, same brand palette, same device angle, same quiet premium mood. They should look credible as product visuals, not generic stock photos and not playful language-game artwork.

Output each image at 1400 x 1800 px, portrait, clean enough to be used as a background with a dark gradient overlay. Keep the bottom 30% calm and low-detail so white overlay text remains readable. Do not place large readable headlines or marketing text inside the image. Do not include English words. If Arabic appears, it should be small UI content or abstract Arabic text lines, not a big headline.

Brand feel:

- Arabic-first learning product
- Calm, focused, intelligent, warm
- Premium but approachable
- Modern interface, not corporate stock
- Colors: deep green, soft mint, off-white paper, restrained gold accents, dark ink
- Rounded UI surfaces, subtle shadows, crisp typography-like line work
- Meaning-preserving reading, vocabulary learning, gradual progress

Composition:

- Portrait 7:9
- Main visual action in the upper and middle area
- Lower third intentionally quieter because the page overlays copy there
- Works under a dark green-to-transparent gradient overlay
- No important details at the extreme edges because the page uses object-cover

Technical:

- photorealistic product mockup mixed with refined editorial UI illustration
- high-resolution, sharp, polished
- realistic glass/device reflections but not distracting
- no visible brand claims, no fake partner logos, no fake testimonials
- no large text baked into the image

## Image 1: Clear Arabic

Generate a portrait product visual for Waddeh showing the idea of "Clear Arabic."

Scene:

A modern tablet or laptop screen sits on a quiet study desk, angled slightly toward the viewer. On the screen is an Arabic reading interface: a central article card with Arabic paragraph lines becoming clearer and more spacious. Some words are softly highlighted in mint green and pale gold to suggest simplified phrasing and comprehension support. The interface should feel like an actual reading tool, with structured text blocks, subtle controls, and a calm learning workspace.

Visual metaphor:

The original Arabic text feels approachable without being childish. Show layered translucent reading cards or subtle before/after text layers, where the front layer is clearer and easier to read. The learner is being met at their current level.

Details:

- Use Arabic-like lines or small authentic Arabic snippets only as interface texture; no big readable headline
- Soft off-white paper surface, deep green sidebar or header accent, mint highlights
- One or two highlighted phrases in the paragraph area
- A small unobtrusive level indicator may appear as simple dots or steps, without readable labels
- The image should feel calm, focused, and trustworthy
- Leave the bottom 30% less busy, with darker green tones or soft desk blur for overlay readability

Mood:

Quiet confidence, clarity, first step into Arabic reading.

Avoid:

Generic books-only imagery, students smiling at camera, cartoon owl/mascot, giant text, English UI labels, clutter.

## Image 2: Bridge to Original

Generate a portrait product visual for Waddeh showing the idea of "Bridge to Original."

Scene:

A refined Arabic reading interface displays two related text layers: a clearer Arabic version and a more authentic original version. They are connected by a subtle visual bridge: thin mint lines, matching highlighted phrases, or a soft split-view transition. The screen should show that meaning is preserved while the learner gradually moves closer to the source text.

Visual metaphor:

Two Arabic text cards overlap or sit side by side. The left/front card is simpler and more spacious; the right/back card is denser and more authentic. Highlighted segments align between them, suggesting "this phrase maps to that phrase." The connection should be elegant, not technical or diagram-heavy.

Details:

- Use deep green and off-white as the dominant background
- Use restrained gold to highlight a phrase that changes between the clear and original versions
- Include faint connector lines or a soft arc between matching phrases
- Interface should look like a real product screen, not a whiteboard diagram
- No large readable headline inside the image
- No English words
- Keep the lower 30% visually quiet and readable under overlay text

Mood:

Progress, trust, preserved meaning, gentle movement toward authentic Arabic.

Avoid:

Literal bridge architecture, translation app look, dictionary-only interface, random bilingual text, busy charts.

## Image 3: Reading Memory

Generate a portrait product visual for Waddeh showing the idea of "Reading Memory."

Scene:

A modern product screen shows a learner's saved Arabic vocabulary and reading progress. There are compact vocabulary cards, small mastery indicators, and a reading memory timeline or progress rail. The visual should suggest that Waddeh remembers what the learner has read, which words they are learning, and how their Arabic level adapts over time.

Visual metaphor:

Vocabulary and meaning build gently over time. Show saved word cards emerging from a reading document, with subtle connections back to highlighted words in the text. Include small progress markers or level steps that feel mature and restrained.

Details:

- Arabic vocabulary cards can show short Arabic words or abstract Arabic line shapes
- Use small badges, progress dots, and memory cards in mint, green, off-white, and restrained gold
- The UI should feel useful and real, not gamified
- A few cards can float or layer above the reading surface, but keep everything organized
- No large readable headline inside the image
- No English words
- Keep the bottom 30% calmer and darker for overlay copy

Mood:

Accumulated learning, gentle progress, personal reading memory, confidence returning to Arabic.

Avoid:

Flashcard app clone, childish trophies, streak counters, fake stats, confetti, busy dashboard overload.
