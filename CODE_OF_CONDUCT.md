# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Waddeh contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, language, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific in feedback.
- Respect Arabic language varieties, learner backgrounds, and differing levels of expertise.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Protect private learner content, credentials, and personal information.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and help others participate safely.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety or effectiveness of the community.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive behavior. For sensitive conduct reports, use a private GitHub channel when available and do not publish personal details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly, fairly, and confidentially as the available project tools permit.
